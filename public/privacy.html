Privacy Policy

Hour 25 · Last updated Sep 2026

Hour 25 is a personal time-tracking app run by an individual, not a company. This policy describes what it stores, why, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

Hour 25 is operated by cayan3. For any questions about your data, or to request deletion, email me at contact@hour25.app.

What is collected

When you sign in with Google:

  • Your email address and a Google account identifier, used to identify your account.
  • Basic profile information Google returns during sign-in (e.g. your name and profile picture). The app itself only ever displays your email address, which is visible only to you.

What you enter into the app:

  • Time entries: a date, a half-hour slot, which label you assigned, and any note you wrote.
  • Your labels and categories: their names and colours.
  • Your settings: sleep window, theme preference, the timezone your browser reports, and when you completed setup.

Stored in your browser, not sent anywhere:

  • A queue of changes that haven't synced yet, so logging works offline.
  • A cached copy of your own data, so the app can open even when offline.
  • The following preferences: your recently used labels, your chosen theme, which hints you don't want to see again.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of this from your device. Anything that's already been synced is kept in the cloud.

What isn't collected

  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party trackers, pixels, or embedded scripts.
  • Your data is never sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

Why it is collected

Solely to provide the app: to sign you in, to store what you log, and to show it back to you. There is no secondary use. Your entries are not read, analysed, or used to train anything.

Where it's stored

Three services are involved:

  • Supabase — hosts the database holding your account and entries.
  • Google — handles sign-in only.
  • Cloudflare — serves the app's user-agnostic files.

Your data is protected by database row-level security, which restricts every record to the account that created it.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. There's no automatic expiry, since old entries are the point of the app. Deleting your account removes everything associated with it.

Your choices

  • Export. Settings → Backup downloads a complete copy of everything in your account as a JSON file, at any time, without asking anyone.
  • Correction. Everything you entered can be edited or cleared in the app.
  • Deletion. Settings → Your data → Delete my account deletes your account and everything in it, at any time, without asking anyone. Settings → Your data → Reset my data deletes your entries, labels, and categories but keeps the account so you can start over. For any questions and concerns, email contact@hour25.app for a response within a few days and at most within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to processing or to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Cookies

No advertising or analytics cookies are used. The app stores a sign-in session and the local data described above in your browser so that it can keep you signed in and work offline.

Children

Hour 25 is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them.

Changes

Any policy changes will be noted in the app.
